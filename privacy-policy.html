Privacy Policy

Mela — Transaction Tracker · Version 0.1.0 · Effective October 5, 2026

Summary
  • Mela stores your financial records only on your device. We have no servers and no cloud database.
  • We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data.
  • There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Backups are encrypted with a password only you know, and are saved only where you choose.

1. About this policy

This policy explains how the Mela mobile app ("Mela", "the app", "we", "us") handles information. Mela is a personal transaction tracker that helps you record and review your income and spending. It is designed to work entirely offline, on your device.

Mela is published by [Publisher / developer name]. Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact address in section 11.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any information. Mela does not transmit data from your device to us or to anyone else. We cannot see, access or recover anything you enter into the app.

3. Information stored on your device

To work, Mela keeps the information you enter in a local database on your device:

  • Accounts you create (for example, names you give to your bank accounts, wallets or cash)
  • Transactions you record (amounts, dates, categories, notes)
  • Categories and app settings

This information stays on your device. It is not uploaded, synchronized or shared. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for Mela, so your records are not copied to a cloud backup without your action.

4. Device permissions

This version of Mela does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or text messages, and the Android app does not request internet access.

When you export or back up your data, or restore a backup, Mela uses your device's standard system file picker. Mela can only access the specific file or folder you select there.

5. Backups and exports

FeatureWhat it doesProtection
Encrypted backup (.mela file) Saves a full copy of your data to a location you choose, so you can restore it later or on a new device. Encrypted with a password you choose (Argon2id key derivation and AES-256-GCM). The password is never stored. If you lose it, the backup cannot be recovered by you or anyone else.
Export (CSV or JSON) Creates a readable file for use in spreadsheets or other apps. Not encrypted. Anyone who has the file can read it, so store and share it carefully.

If you save a backup or export to a cloud storage service (for example, through the file picker), that service handles the file under its own terms and privacy policy, not ours.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, share or disclose your information to anyone, because we never receive it. Mela contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time by:

  • deleting individual records in the app;
  • clearing the app's storage in your device settings; or
  • uninstalling the app.

Backup and export files you created are separate files. They stay wherever you saved them until you delete them. Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot delete or recover it for you.

8. Security

Your data is protected by your device's own security, such as your screen lock and device encryption. We recommend that you keep a screen lock enabled, keep your device up to date, and use a strong, unique password for backups.

9. Children's privacy

Mela is not directed at children under 13. Because Mela does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add new features. If a future version handles information differently, we will update this page and change the effective date above before that version is released. Any new feature that needs additional access to your device will ask for your permission first.
